I build computing systems that make better use of data, hardware, and computation. My research sits at the intersection of storage and memory systems, AI infrastructure, and resilient computing—with a focus on turning new systems ideas into practical capabilities.

My group studies how systems can adapt to changing workloads, make informed resource decisions, and recover reliably across classical and emerging quantum-computing environments. I also work on neuro-symbolic AI tools that support STEM advising and learning.

Research agenda

Systems that adapt, optimize, and recover

I look for opportunities where changes in device behavior, system architecture, and application needs can be brought together to enable computing that is more efficient, dependable, and useful.

01 / STORAGE & MEMORY

Rethinking the data path

Designing storage and memory systems that reduce data movement, improve I/O behavior, and make indexing and resource management more efficient across heterogeneous devices.

02 / AI INFRASTRUCTURE

Systems for data- and compute-intensive AI

Building infrastructure for AI training and inference, including checkpointing, model versioning, data preprocessing, and efficient use of CPU, GPU, memory, and storage resources.

03 / RESILIENT COMPUTING

Reliability across classical and quantum systems

Developing checkpointing and recovery techniques for long-running workloads, including quantum and hybrid quantum–classical applications operating under noisy, resource-constrained conditions.

04 / NEURO-SYMBOLIC AI

AI that supports STEM learning and advising

Combining language models with structured knowledge and symbolic reasoning to build more grounded, context-aware tools for student advising and educational decision-making.

I am a computer systems researcher and educator at Florida International University’s Knight Foundation School of Computing and Information Sciences. Before joining FIU, I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from Northeastern University and worked in industry as a performance architect intern with Samsung Semiconductor.
